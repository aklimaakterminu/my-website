How a sales conversation with me goes.

From a first message to a big package, I follow the same five steps.

  1. Listen

    I start with your background, goals and hesitations, not a pitch.

  2. Diagnose

    I work out what’s really holding things back.

  3. Educate

    I explain the options honestly, including what you would still need to do yourself.

  4. Recommend

    I suggest what actually fits.

  5. Convert

    When it’s clearly the right fit, I help you say yes with confidence.

02 · Pre-order online store, China and Malaysia to Bangladesh

Mirora BD

A Shein-style pre-order store that makes buying from China and Malaysia feel safe.

  • Brand
  • E-commerce website
  • Content
The idea
Shoppers in Bangladesh want products from China and Malaysia, but paying in full for something that hasn’t arrived is a risk. Mirora lets them pre-order with 30% now and pay the rest on delivery, in 15 to 30 days. If the item can’t be sourced, the advance is refunded.
What I built
A mobile-first store with Shein-style categories, search, wishlist, guest checkout or an account with reviews, and a welcome offer. Behind it sits an admin where my partner and I add products and update availability, with order alerts for WhatsApp and Telegram, and payment by bKash or bank.
My part
The brand and the look: burgundy, cream and dusty rose, home banners made from clean product photos with editable text on top, a clear four-step “how pre-order works” section and a caption template for Facebook and Instagram.

Stories from the sales floor.

Transfotech Academy, Senior Career Advisor

A generic course, rebuilt around what the market wanted.

Listen to the market before trying to sell harder.

The challenge
The Web Security program was fairly generic, while the market was moving towards cybersecurity engineering combined with AI.
What I did
I researched competitors and proposed a new product. With the instructor and team I worked out what could realistically be taught, how AI would fit, the length, the pricing and the sales approach. Then I designed the course and its brochure.
The result
It became the academy’s top course, with the strongest performance, enrollment and revenue.

Transfotech Academy, Senior Career Advisor

One honest conversation, two courses.

Good sales conversations leave the customer clearer than when they started.

The challenge
A prospect in the US, with a Grade 12 education and no bachelor’s degree, was interested in cybersecurity but doubted he could build a career in it. His real concern was uncertainty, not course choice.
What I did
I started with his background, goals and hesitations before presenting anything. I made no unrealistic promises, explained the learning paths and what he would still need to do himself, and recommended two programs that worked together.
The result
He enrolled in both courses.

Hirespan, Founder

From the first ad to the first paying customer.

Win one customer properly, then repeat what worked.

The challenge
A brand-new business with no reviews, a small following and a small ad budget.
What I did
I built the website, the social pages and the content first, so the ads had something credible to land on. Then I ran Meta ads aimed at job seekers in Malaysia and handled the enquiries on WhatsApp, talking each person through the options before recommending a service.
